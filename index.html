Meet Axel!

You only need two thumbs.

↔

Slide to move

Slide the stick on the left. Let go to stop.

◎

Tap to do things

The big right button tells you what to do. Walk closer if it says “Go closer”.

≈

Water is your friend

Swim to fill your blue water bar. Hide in bushes when guards come near.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move · E or Space to act

How to Play

💦

Keep Hydrated!

Your hydration drains on dry land. Dive into lakes, pools, puddles, or drink soda to stay wet.

🕵️‍♂️

Be Stealthy

Avoid the vision cones of lifeguards and guests. Hide in bushes or coolers when they get close.

🍕

Steal & Sell

Snatch food from coolers and trash. Eat it for speed boosts, or sell it to the Sewer Rat for Shiny Pebbles!

🪖

Get the Helmet!

Save up enough Pebbles to buy the ultimate upgrade: a Water Helmet that lets you walk on land forever!
